# OpsKestrel Positioning

## Name

`Ops` means operations. A kestrel is a small falcon known for hovering,
observing, and making precise dives. OpsKestrel is therefore positioned as an
operator that watches cross-system work, identifies exceptions, takes bounded
actions, and preserves evidence.

The name is a coined product name and is not a claim of trademark exclusivity.

## Product Category

OpsKestrel is an **evidence-first operations automation platform for macOS and
browser workflows**.

It is not positioned as a general accounting ledger, a cloud CMDB, or a generic
chat-driven desktop agent. Invoice reconciliation is the first reference
workflow used to demonstrate the platform.

## Differentiation

| Area | OpsKestrel focus |
|---|---|
| Execution | Deterministic desktop actions and browser DOM adapters |
| Decisions | Business rules decide outcomes; visual models verify evidence |
| Recovery | Per-record isolation, retries, checkpoints, and resumable batches |
| Governance | Operator tokens, role thresholds, batch review, and review closure |
| Audit | Hash-chained review events, content fingerprints, and evidence ZIPs |
| Deployment | Local macOS console, queue workers, LaunchAgent, and PostgreSQL |
| Evidence | JSON, HTML, XLSX, screenshots, diagnostics, and SHA-256 manifests |

Other projects may share words such as ledger, operations, audit, or
reconciliation. OpsKestrel must differentiate through the complete combination
of desktop/browser execution, deterministic rules, resumable operation, human
review, and tamper-evident evidence.

## Public Description

> OpsKestrel is an evidence-first operations automation platform for macOS and
> browser workflows. It combines deterministic execution, business rules,
> resumable batch processing, human review, and auditable evidence.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not claim the first or only operations ledger.
- Do not claim production validation against SAP, Yonyou, Kingdee, or a real
  customer ERP without a completed deployment.
- Do not imply that the vision model makes accounting decisions.
- Do not claim legal trademark exclusivity based on public search results.
- Do not remove upstream MIT attribution.
